> **System 1 Autonomous Edge Decision Engine**

---

## 🧭 Brand Vision & Philosophy

Sys1Pop's brand identity reflects the convergence of **retro computer craftsmanship** and **split-second autonomous edge inference**:

* **"System 1" Cognition**: Inspired by Daniel Kahneman's cognitive theory (*Thinking, Fast and Slow*), System 1 represents fast, instinctive, sub-conscious, autonomous decision-making. In Sys1Pop, this is embodied by the glowing vintage **Nixie tube `1`** (a sudden warm filament flash of insight) and microsecond V8 isolate execution.
* **"PoP" Edge Presence**: Point of Presence edge routing. Represented visually through **popping cyber bubbles** and concentric sonar/radar waves radiating outwards across global edge nodes.
* **Retro-Robotic Mascot**: A quirky, friendly CRT-monitor robot with round nerd goggles, communicating approachable intelligence, deep technical engineering, and nostalgic computing roots.

## 📐 Usage Guidelines & Best Practices

1. **Clear Space**: Always allow breathing room around the emblems and wordmarks equal to at least the height of the robot goggles.
2. **Text Pairing**: When pairing the emblem with the wordmark manually, ensure the horizontal baseline of `SYS1POP` aligns with the lower half of the shield.
3. **Contrast**: Use `sys1pop_banner_dark.jpg` whenever embedding into dark-mode Markdown documents (e.g. GitHub `README.md` with dark previewers) and `sys1pop_banner_light.jpg` on light backgrounds.
4. **No Distortion**: Maintain original aspect ratios (1:1 for emblems, 16:9 for banners, 3:2 for wordmark) without stretching or non-uniform scaling.
